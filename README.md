## 📌 Overview

This repository contains the complete source code, assets, documentation, and handover materials for the **MChem Consultants** digital platform. Engineered from the ground up to match the world-class **NIW (niwnet.com)** design language, it pairs sleek dark/light dual-mode aesthetics with high-performance responsive engineering.

---

## 🌟 Core Features & Modules

1. **2-Second Brand Splash Reveal Animation**: Smooth intro overlay featuring a rotating glowing logo, official tagline, and animated progress bar with 60fps auto-dismiss.
2. **Floating Island Navbar**: Fixed glassmorphism island navbar with enlarged logo + bold capital branding `MCHEM CONSULTANTS`, Email Us action pill, light/dark mode switch, and responsive slide drawer.
3. **Hero Section with Looping Background Video**: Deep headroom layout with ambient engineering video (`bgvideomchem.mp4`), cross-browser autoplay guarantee, and protective radial gradient.
4. **About & Our Story**: Bento card layout + standalone page (`page-about.php`) highlighting BIC MUET incubation, official mission statement, and official BIC logo badge.
5. **Moving Capabilities Ticker**: Infinite smooth horizontal ticker; all Aspen HYSYS branding replaced with *"Simulation and Modeling"*.
6. **6 Core Engineering Services**: Process Simulation & Modeling, Sustainable Solutions, Process Safety & HAZOP, Plant Design & P&ID, Energy Recovery & Pinch Integration, and CPD Corporate Training.
7. **Projects & Case Studies**: Interactive case study bento dashboards + notice for 20+ executed industrial assignments.
8. **Photo & Video Gallery**: 3-column responsive grid with authentic chemical engineering imagery and full-screen lightbox modal.
9. **Target Industries Fan Deck**: Curated for Oil & Gas, Chemical, Textile, and Academic sectors with dynamic scope inspection.
10. **Leadership Team Carousel**: Unclipped squircle avatar cards for all 5 team members with dual contact: direct WhatsApp and direct Email buttons on every card.
11. **Client Reviews & Testimonials**: 5.0-star rated reviews section featuring verified endorsements from plant managers at SSGC, Lucky Cement, Indus Textile, and QUEST.
12. **Consultation Booking Form**: Searchable country code selector (30+ countries), drag-and-drop file upload zone (up to 10MB), and dual instant dispatch (WhatsApp + Email).
13. **Dual Floating Quick Contact Dock**: Fixed bottom-right dock featuring **Email Us** and **WhatsApp** side-by-side with smart webmail redirection.
14. **Enterprise SEO & Google Search Protection**: JSON-LD `ProfessionalService` rich snippets, OpenGraph social sharing preview, keyword tags, and automated **HTTP 410 Gone** engine to purge legacy spam/demo URLs.
15. **Live Chat & AI (Tidio)**: Built-in Customizer control allowing instant activation of Tidio chat and AI bot via project key.
16. **100% Client Dashboard Autonomy**: Full control to edit text, upload photos, manage case studies, update team members, and add client reviews.

## 🚀 Installation & Deployment

1. In WordPress Admin, navigate to **Appearance → Themes**.
2. Click **Add New Theme** → **Upload Theme**.
3. Choose `mchemconsultants-pro.zip` (or upload the `mchemconsultants-theme/` folder via FTP/SSH into `/wp-content/themes/`).
4. Click **Install Now** and **Activate**.
5. Customize hero text, mission, contact info, and SEO under **Appearance → Customize → MChem Theme Options**.
