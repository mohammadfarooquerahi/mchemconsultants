Section 03

Client Administration & Content Management Guide

The MChem platform is built for non-technical administrators. The client can edit every piece of text, upload project photos, and manage inquiries directly from the WordPress Admin Dashboard.

1Editing Hero Text, Mission Statement & Contact Info

  1. Navigate to WordPress Admin → Appearance → Customize → MChem Theme Options.
  2. Hero Section: Modify the main headline, tagline ("Engineering Sustainable Solutions for better tomorrow"), and button texts.
  3. Contact & WhatsApp: Update the phone number, email (mchemconsultants2@gmail.com), and office address.
  4. About & Credibility: Edit the Mission Statement and About Story in real time with live preview.
  5. Click Publish to push changes live instantly.

2Uploading Photos & Videos to the Gallery

  1. Go to WordPress Admin → Gallery → Add New Gallery Item.
  2. Enter the title/caption (e.g., “Industrial Plant Visit at Kotri Site”).
  3. In the right sidebar under Featured Image, click Set featured image and upload your photo.
  4. (Optional) If you have an engineering video, paste the YouTube, Vimeo, or MP4 link in the video field.
  5. Click Publish. The new photo will appear automatically in the homepage Gallery with full-screen lightbox zoom!

3Managing Leadership Team Members

  1. Go to WordPress Admin → Team → All Team Members.
  2. Click on any member (e.g., Muhsin Ali, Sardar Ali) or click Add New Team Member.
  3. Update their bio, designation (e.g., Founder & Managing Director), and upload their official headshot under Featured Image.
  4. Click Update. The team carousel updates automatically.

4Publishing New Projects & Case Studies

  1. Go to WordPress Admin → Projects → Add New Project.
  2. Enter the project title, engineering scope, client name, and operational metrics.
  3. Click Publish. The project immediately appears in the case study showcase.

5Adding Client Reviews & Testimonials

  1. Go to WordPress Admin → Reviews → Add New Review.
  2. Enter the reviewer's name in the Title (e.g., Engr. Tariq Mehmood).
  3. Type their testimonial quote in the main content box.
  4. Enter their role, company name, and star rating in the custom fields, then click Publish.
Section 04

Google Search Diagnosis & SEO Resolution Action Plan

1. Root Cause Analysis of Unrelated Search Results

The client reported that searching for mchem consultants on Google showed strange links:
• mchemconsultants.com/stockroom/... ("Free Easy Menu Editor Premium WordPress Plugin Download")
• mchemconsultants.com/stockroom/maroon-p... ("Download Maroon – Photography Pro Theme Free")
• Sitelinks: Seele, Legenda, Aboss, Responsive Clothing, Download DC Agency

Why This Happened: Prior to installing the clean MChem theme, the WordPress hosting environment contained old demo content, nulled plugins, or dummy downloads in the database. Googlebot crawled those pages earlier and indexed them. When the MChem theme was activated, the theme's global footer rendered across all pages, causing Google to associate the old post titles with MChem's description.

2. Theme Engine Fix Implemented in v32.0.0 (inc/seo.php)

  • Automated HTTP 410 Gone Purge: Any request to old dummy or spam paths (stockroom, maroon-p, easy-menu-editor, seele, legenda, aboss, dc-agency) is intercepted with an immediate 410 Content Removed and X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive. According to Google's official documentation, 410 Gone is the fastest signal instructing Googlebot to permanently drop those obsolete URLs.
  • JSON-LD Structured Data Schema: Outputs rich snippets declaring MChem Consultants as an accredited ProfessionalService and Organization at MUET Jamshoro.
  • Social Graph Integration: Open Graph and Twitter Cards ensure that whenever a link to mchemconsultants.com is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or Facebook, it renders a high-resolution branded preview card.
Fast 2-Step Action for the Client to Speed Up Google Search Update:
  1. Delete Old Dummy Posts in WordPress:
    Go to WP Admin → Posts → All Posts. Select all dummy posts, choose Move to Trash, then open the Trash tab and click Empty Trash. Do the same under Pages.
  2. Submit URL Removal in Google Search Console:
    Open Google Search Console → Click Removals → Click New Request → Select Remove all URLs with this prefix → Enter: https://mchemconsultants.com/stockroom/ and submit. Google will clear these URLs from search results within 24–48 hours.
